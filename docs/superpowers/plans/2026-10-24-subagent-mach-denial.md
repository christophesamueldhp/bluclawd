## Review Focus

1. **Stripped or falsely advertised capability:** an old schema drops the field, or schema/types advertise it while the executed generator ignores it; refuse the former and keep real acceptance red for the latter (Tasks 2/3).
2. **Override and drift loopholes:** `[]`, caller arrays changed during an asynchronous wrap, exact/wildcard allows or a session update must not silently erase required denials or mutate parent state (Tasks 1/3).
3. **Literal confusion:** UTF-8 byte length, NUL/control characters, `*`, quotes/backslashes and SBPL-looking text must not alter matching or inject rules (Task 1).
4. **False negative oracle:** unavailable broker, failed build/probe, worker startup failure or timeout must not be reported as an enforced denial; parent positive controls remain meaningful (Task 3).
5. **Dependency provenance and tooling:** correct declarations but wrong built JS, unreviewed transitive changes, lifecycle hooks or unavailable test tools must block adoption rather than cause silent installs or test bypasses (Tasks 1/2).
